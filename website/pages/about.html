Why I Built This Alone—And Who I Built It For

I am a one-person team on this mission. Every line of JavaScript, every procedural mechanic, every papercut sprite concept, and every NASA data integration in CRASHLANDED was built solo from the ground up. Taking on a project of this scale alone during a hackathon is an enormous challenge—but I do this because I genuinely love what I create.

Even though I am coding at this desk by myself today, I am never truly alone. I built this for all the people who aren't physically here with me today, yet continue to believe in my work, my ideas, and my craft. Their trust is the fuel that kept this outpost running through every late night.

"Just like the Apollo astronaut who left a family photograph on the dusty surface of the Moon, this project is my own mementos left on the digital frontier—a reminder that behind all technology and science, love and human connection are why we explore."
// THE DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

A GAME FOR 12-YEAR-OLDS THAT I GENUINELY LOVE TO PLAY

Bridging the gap between accessible STEM learning and authentic indie gaming.

INSPIRATION_01 // THE GAMEPLAY

Born from Classics I Admire

When I read the challenge brief for ages 7–12, I knew I didn't want to build a watered-down educational quiz that talks down to kids. My goal was personal: to create a game that young learners can grasp immediately, but that I—as a gamer and developer—would genuinely enjoy playing for hours.

That vision led me to fuse the deep colony management and psychological stakes of RimWorld with the charming, hand-drawn 2.5D papercut aesthetic of Don't Starve Together, plus historical Easter eggs inspired by classic LEGO collectibles.

INSPIRATION_02 // THE HEART

Crafted with Purpose & Detail

Even the Outpost Commander you control on screen is a playful caricature of myself—wearing my everyday navy shirt, khaki pants, and white sneakers—dropped onto an alien world.

By offering both a streamlined Cadet Protocol (Oxygen, Power, Shielding) and an unforgiving Commander Protocol (adding Hunger, Hydroponics, and Mental Sanity), CRASHLANDED respects the intelligence of kids while delivering a real survival challenge for everyone else.
