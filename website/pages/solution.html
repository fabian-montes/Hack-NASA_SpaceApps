// TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE & GAMEPLAY

THE SOLUTION: CRASHLANDED

A zero-install, browser-playable 2.5D survival simulator transforming real NASA aerospace engineering constraints into an engaging experience.

// 01. CORE DESIGN PRINCIPLE

ZERO FREE CHOICES

Every action carries a real resource cost that compounds over time, mirroring authentic mission planning[cite: 7].

RESOURCE BALANCE OXYGEN, POWER & SHIELDING

Diverting power to electromagnetic shielding during a solar storm protects your crew from radiation, but freezes hydroponic crops and drains life support[cite: 1, 7].

ACCESSIBILITY ZERO BARRIER TO ENTRY

Built with vanilla ES6 modules and Phaser 3 on GitHub Pages. Instant browser play with no downloads, accounts, or heavy hardware required[cite: 2, 4, 7].

DUAL AUDIENCE SCALABLE DIFFICULTY

Intuitive tooltips and 3 core systems for younger players, scaling up to full psychological, hunger, and hydroponic management in Hardcore Mode[cite: 1, 7].

// 02. GAMEPLAY VARIANTS

CHOOSE YOUR PERSPECTIVE

Two distinct operational variants derived from the core narrative of a mission gone wrong[cite: 5].

MODE_01 // THE SURVIVORS

Trek to the Extraction Point

Stranded after a catastrophic crash landing, your crew must stay healthy, brave real solar storms, and complete science and resource missions while navigating across hazardous procedural terrain toward the extraction coordinates[cite: 5].

MODE_02 // THE RESCUE SQUAD

Race Against the Clock

Deploy with strictly limited supplies to locate your missing comrades before their life support fails. Master real space mission logistics, ration every watt of power, and chart the unknown[cite: 5].

// 03. ENGINEERING INFRASTRUCTURE

MODULAR TECH STACK

Engineered with clean ES6 architecture and strict adherence to DRY (Don't Repeat Yourself) principles[cite: 2].

01

Phaser 3 Engine

Lightweight 2.5D rendering utilizing Y-depth sorting (`setDepth`) and smooth camera follow mechanics without complex 3D overhead[cite: 2, 4].

02

NASA Open Data Integration

Dynamic event triggers backed by NASA's DONKI (space weather/solar flares) and OSDR (biological stress metrics)[cite: 1, 5, 7].

03

Procedural Generation

Simplex-noise world generation distributing interactive terrain, craters, and historical space artifacts seamlessly[cite: 2, 4].

04

Data-Driven Design

Decoupled JSON configurations for structures, entities, and easter eggs allowing rapid balance tuning and educational tooltips[cite: 2, 5].
