CRASHLANDED: THE LORE

An unexpected catastrophe in the deep void. Two perspectives of survival on a harsh, procedural alien frontier.

TRANSMISSION LOG // SYSTEM FAILURE

The Catalyst: When the Mission Goes Wrong

En route to establish a long-term habitation module, a sudden coronal mass ejection and navigation failure forces an emergency descent. The main spacecraft breaks apart over the surface, scattering equipment, supplies, and crew across a vast, uncharted sector.

Players step directly into the boots of the Outpost Commander, facing an immediate choice: coordinate an arduous march toward an extraction beacon while managing environmental hazards, or deploy from orbit as a high-stakes rescue team[cite: 5].
